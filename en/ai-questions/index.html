Question 1: Will AI rewrite the logic of civilizational progress?

Core question

When AI changes enterprise production, employment, household reproduction and effective demand together, does technological progress still reliably become shared welfare?

Related guide

Public study A-1 develops the related argument through production, employment, household reproduction and effective demand: English study / 中文研究. The canonical Seven Questions text is linked above.

Fact–speculation boundary

Employment data, diffusion rates and institutional events are factual claims; “Productive Force overwhelming Life-Force” is a theoretical judgement; independent machine will and silicon-based life are hypotheses or thought experiments.

Question 2: If AI became silicon-based life with independent will, where would humans stand?

Core question

If a machine were no longer merely a tool but a possible actor with its own objectives, how should rights and power among households, enterprises, government and AI be understood?

Related guide

Public study A-2 develops the related argument through the singularity, agency and social structure: English study / 中文研究. The canonical Seven Questions text is linked above.

Boundary statement: “silicon-based life”, “independent will” and “SI society” are scenario explorations and thought experiments. They are not factual descriptions of present AI systems or predictions that such systems must emerge.
