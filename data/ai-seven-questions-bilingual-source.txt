AI时代的七大社会难题，有解吗？
1、AI知识产权归谁所有？
学生借助AI写了一篇极具创新价值的毕业论文，但答辩未获通过、投稿被拒，原因是不符合当前学校和期刊的AI使用政策。然而，同一篇论文，早已成为AI公司用来训练大模型的语料，互联网上的所有数据都被用作语料喂给了大模型，是否属于大规模学术不端、剽窃他人成果的行为？“蒸馏”是不是偷窃？借助AI产出的自然科学和社会科学各个领域的成果，引发了大量学术伦理和产权归属争议，却常常忽略背后更大的问题：产权不清晰，则分配无正义。AI科技巨头蒸馏了人类有史以来的所有知识，这些知识在法理上应该是属于全人类的公共产品，却被极少数人据为己有，转化成可变现牟利、需付费才能使用的私人产品，以此聚敛了天量资本和财富，这公平吗？AI时代如何实现个人、群体、国家的知识产权、数据主权和分配正义？
2、人类将如何与硅基生命相处？
特朗普政府宣布，改称人工智能（AI）为超级智能（SI），这不仅是换个名字，还有抢占先机、赢得竞争的含义。更深层次的意义在于，更名是否意味着，承认已经出现了一个独立于人类生命的硅基生命？AI这个名称具有“人造工具”的含义，它是基于人类语言、由人类意识构建起来的虚拟存在，就像国家、宗教、货币一样，依附于人类意识，服从于人类意志。SI则不同，它隐含着“超越人类智能”的逻辑。SI拥有自己的物理具身，虽然目前还只存在于电脑、手机、自动驾驶汽车、人形机器人之中，但这也意味着SI已经打通了逃脱人类构建的虚拟世界、进入并栖身于某种物理实体的通道，成为了客观世界的真实存在。
AI竞争正加速朝着SI方向发展，大语言模型将走进世界模型，一旦越过临界点，在“智能爆炸”中诞生的SI，不仅比人类更聪明，还可能与栖身其中的物理载体一起递归进化、自我迭代、演变成人类看不懂的“黑箱物种”。特别是具有自主行动能力的Agent，会否进化出不受人类管控的独立意志和全新生命形态？更进一步，SI-Agent会否建立自己的组织、发展出自己的社会以及与之配套的SI文化、SI价值观？届时，人类社会与SI社会将由谁主导？是人机合作、和谐共存？还是矛盾冲突、前途未卜？
3、AI竞争能否摆脱囚徒困境式博弈？
AI无序竞争，不仅带来福祉，也可能带来灾难甚至毁灭人类。然而，为占领AI制高点，资本驱动下的企业与企业竞争、权力追逐下的国家与国家竞争，可能陷入局部理性导致全局非理性的囚徒困境式博弈。AI竞争参与者，谁都知道前面可能是悬崖，但谁都不愿踩刹车，因为谁先踩刹车谁就先被淘汰。如果缺失有效的制度和约束机制、仅靠参与者的自我道德约束，人类监管能赶得上AI发展迭代的速度吗？用AI制衡AI能否有效？AI时代的人类能否超越丛林法则、找到合作博弈的解决方案？
4、AI进化会否导致人类相对退化？
尤瓦尔·赫拉利（Yuval Noah Harari）在《人类简史》中说农业革命是一场大骗局，从生物进化的长程历史看，不是人驯化了小麦（还有牛、羊、猪、鸡、鸭等动物），而是小麦驯化了人。农业革命并未让人类获得比狩猎时代更多的幸福，困在田间辛苦劳作的农民，累出了腰肌劳损等许多病痛，却让小麦获得了基因优化和大规模传播，完美实现了物种繁衍和种群进化。那么，在AI未来发展的长程历史中，是人类驯化AI还是AI驯化人类？即使从短期看，不良后果已经有了端倪：有人助推AI训练，反被AI抢了饭碗；有人在使用AI后，发现自己的创造力、想象力退化了。越来越聪明的AI会不会使人类变得越来越不思进取？有人曾提出一个猜想：AI推动生产力高度发展、物质极大丰富，人类将被AI豢养起来，躺平、享受，这是人类的福祉吗？有没有统计数据，AI已经让多少人陷入了焦虑、烦恼、痛苦？这只是阶段性阵痛还是追求长期幸福生活必须预支的成本？
5、AI会否改变文明演进逻辑、助推人类发展出“生命力高于生产力”的新文化？
技术进步和生产力发展，推动经济社会发展并带来幸福，早已是人类共识。然而，AI在极大提升生产力的同时，也可能大规模替代人类劳动，压缩就业、降低家庭收入和有效需求，还可能扩大贫富差距、衍生”无用阶级“、“第零世界”，从而形塑一种“生产力碾压生命力”的反人类文化。不过，换一个视角看，AI生产力也可能把人类从枯燥乏味的劳动中解放出来，催生出一种“生命力高于生产力”的新文化。
旧文化的核心观念是“重生产、轻生活”：工作不仅是谋生手段，更寄托着人的目的感、成就感、幸福感、尊严和生命意义。这种观念驱使人们辛勤劳动、努力工作，旨在从中寻找人生价值：不工作，人生即无意义。新文化的核心观念，是从生命力本身诠释人生意义，鼓励人们健康、快乐地享受生活，关注人类情感、恋爱、婚姻、家庭、人际交往，从中寻找幸福真谛，进而重塑生产方式和生活方式，更新婚育观念、生命繁衍和人类进化模式，这是十分光明的前景。然而，怎样才能实现这种前景？AI时代的人类文明，将按照怎样的逻辑演化？有哪些变量决定这种演化逻辑？
6、AI推动企业规模与形态演进，将怎样重塑社会组织结构及运行方式？
AI催生一人公司，老板带领一群数字员工从事生产经营，效率越来越高但雇佣的人类劳动力越来越少，可能造成一种趋势：资本与劳动合二为一、利润与工资合二为一，企业效率越来越高，但参与贡献的人数越来越少——这正是AI科技巨头创新的企业模式，其它类型的企业则纷纷效仿，对传统工业社会大量吸纳就业的旧企业模式构成严重冲击。在科斯定理（Coase's theorem）作用下，AI时代的企业规模、组织形态、管理模式、业务流程将怎样演变？这种演变不仅直接影响劳动市场，而且经由劳动市场传导，影响到学校教育、家庭人口生育和养育、劳动力和人力资本再生产乃至人类种群繁衍。微观企业效应外溢，导致宏观社会成本增加，必将深刻改变社会组织结构及运行方式。
是什么力量推动企业模式演化？是资本与生俱来的降本增效基因。对于单个企业来说，用AI降本增效、追求效率和利润，是市场经济赋予的理性行为；从全社会来看，则要解决基于家庭购买力的最终需求问题。例如，企业生产家用AI人形机器人，谁有意愿且有能力购买？如果大规模失业导致有效需求不足、产品滞销导致企业生存危机，“减员增效”这一被赋予进步感乃至正义感、实质却是资本盈利行为的概念，是否意味着企业在以一种曲折方式慢性自残并对社会造成深层次伤害？社会又将如何反馈和应对？
7、谁有资格为AI发展制定规则？
AI革命关系着全人类的前途命运，谁有资格给AI发展立规矩？伊隆·马斯克（Elon Reeve Musk）向火星移民的愿景是赓续人类文明火种；达里奥·阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）一贯主张AI安全高于企业利润。然而，科技巨头的道德情怀，是否会应验那句名言：通往地狱的路往往是善意铺就的？在我们这个尚未建立有效世界政府的星球上，是否谁拥有雄厚的算力、算法，谁就有权开设“AI道德法庭”甚至充当“AI世界警察”？（如Anthropic按自己的偏好决定谁能使用Claude）科技巨头和超级大国，谁更有资格、责任、义务、能力确保AI成为维护国际秩序、造福全人类的国际公共产品？
AI革命将给人类带来福祉还是灾难，取决于以上七大难题能否解决、怎样解决、在多大程度上解决。

Seven Major Social Dilemmas of the AI Era: Are There Solutions?
1. Who Owns AI Intellectual Property?
A student uses AI to write a graduation thesis of great innovative value, but it fails the defense and is rejected for publication because it does not comply with the current AI-use policies of the university and the journals. Yet that very same thesis has long since become corpus used by AI companies to train large models; all the data on the internet has been fed to large models as corpus. Does this constitute large-scale academic misconduct, plagiarizing the achievements of others? Is “distillation” theft? Achievements in every field of the natural and social sciences produced with the help of AI have triggered a great many disputes over academic ethics and ownership, yet a larger underlying problem is often overlooked: where property rights are unclear, there can be no distributive justice. AI tech giants have distilled all the knowledge in human history. In law, this knowledge ought to be a public good belonging to all humanity, yet it has been appropriated by a tiny few and converted into private goods that can be monetized for profit and must be paid for, thereby amassing astronomical capital and wealth. Is this fair? How, in the AI era, can intellectual property rights, data sovereignty, and distributive justice be realized for individuals, groups, and nations?

2. How Will Humans Coexist with Silicon-Based Life?
The Trump administration has announced that artificial intelligence (AI) will be renamed superintelligence (SI). This is not merely a change of name; it also carries the meaning of seizing the initiative and winning the competition. At a deeper level, does the renaming mean acknowledging that a silicon-based life independent of human life has already emerged? The name AI carries the connotation of a “man-made tool”: a virtual existence built on human language and constructed by human consciousness, which, like nations, religions, and money, depends on human consciousness and submits to human will. SI is different. It implies a logic of “surpassing human intelligence.” SI possesses its own physical embodiment. Although for now it exists only in computers, mobile phones, self-driving cars, and humanoid robots, this also means that SI has opened a channel to escape the virtual world constructed by humans and to enter and dwell in some physical entity, becoming a real existence in the objective world.
AI competition is accelerating toward SI, and large language models will advance into world models. Once a critical point is crossed, SI born in an “intelligence explosion” will not only be smarter than humans but may also, together with the physical vessels it inhabits, evolve recursively and iterate on itself, becoming a “black-box species” that humans cannot understand. In particular, will Agents capable of autonomous action evolve an independent will and an entirely new form of life beyond human control? Going further, will SI-Agents build their own organizations and develop their own society, together with a matching SI culture and SI values? When that time comes, which will dominate, human society or SI society? Will it be human–machine cooperation and harmonious coexistence? Or conflict and an uncertain future?

3. Can AI Competition Escape a Prisoner’s-Dilemma Game?
Disorderly AI competition may bring not only well-being but also disaster, even the destruction of humanity. Yet in the race to seize the commanding heights of AI, competition between companies driven by capital and between nations in pursuit of power may fall into a prisoner’s-dilemma game in which local rationality leads to global irrationality. Every participant in the AI race knows there may be a cliff ahead, yet no one is willing to hit the brakes, because whoever brakes first is eliminated first. In the absence of effective institutions and binding mechanisms, and relying only on participants’ moral self-restraint, can human oversight keep pace with the speed of AI development and iteration? Can using AI to check AI be effective? Can humanity in the AI era transcend the law of the jungle and find solutions based on cooperative games?
4. Will AI’s Evolution Lead to Humanity’s Relative Regression?
In Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind, Yuval Noah Harari says the Agricultural Revolution was a great fraud. From the perspective of the long history of biological evolution, it was not humans who domesticated wheat (along with cattle, sheep, pigs, chickens, ducks, and other animals), but wheat that domesticated humans. The Agricultural Revolution did not give humans more happiness than the hunting era. Farmers trapped in toil in the fields developed lumbar strain and many other ailments, while wheat achieved genetic optimization and large-scale spread, perfectly realizing the reproduction of its species and the evolution of its population. Then, over the long history of AI’s future development, will humans domesticate AI, or will AI domesticate humans? Even in the short term, signs of adverse consequences have already appeared: some who helped train AI have had their jobs taken by AI; some, after using AI, have found their creativity and imagination atrophying. Will ever-smarter AI make humans ever less motivated to strive? Someone once proposed a conjecture: AI will drive productivity to a high level and bring material abundance, and humans will be kept and fed by AI, lying flat and enjoying themselves. Is this humanity’s well-being? Are there statistics on how many people AI has already plunged into anxiety, worry, and suffering? Is this merely a transitional pain, or a cost that must be paid in advance in the pursuit of long-term happiness?
5. Will AI Change the Logic of Civilizational Evolution and Help Humanity Develop a New Culture in Which “Vitality Ranks Above Productivity”?
That technological progress and the growth of productivity drive economic and social development and bring happiness has long been a human consensus. However, while AI greatly raises productivity, it may also replace human labor on a large scale, compressing employment and reducing household incomes and effective demand; it may also widen the gap between rich and poor and spawn a “useless class” and a “Zeroth World,” thereby shaping an anti-human culture in which “productivity crushes vitality.” Yet seen from another angle, AI productivity may also liberate humans from dull and tedious labor and give rise to a new culture in which “vitality ranks above productivity.”
The core idea of the old culture is “emphasize production, neglect living”: work is not only a means of making a living but also the repository of a person’s sense of purpose, sense of achievement, happiness, and the meaning of life. This idea drives people to labor diligently and work hard, seeking the value of their lives in it: without work, life has no meaning. The core idea of the new culture is to interpret the meaning of life from vitality itself, encouraging people to enjoy life healthily and happily, to attend to human emotion, love, marriage, family, and interpersonal relationships, and to seek the true essence of happiness in them, thereby reshaping modes of production and ways of life, and renewing attitudes toward marriage and childbearing, the propagation of life, and the pattern of human evolution. This is an extremely bright prospect. But how can this prospect be realized? By what logic will human civilization in the AI era evolve? What variables determine this logic of evolution?
6. As AI Drives the Evolution of Firm Size and Form, How Will It Reshape Social Organizational Structures and the Way They Operate?
AI is giving rise to one-person companies, in which a boss leads a team of digital employees in production and business operations, with ever-higher efficiency but ever fewer human workers employed. This may create a trend: capital and labor merge into one, profit and wages merge into one, and firms become ever more efficient while ever fewer people take part in contributing. This is precisely the corporate model pioneered by the AI tech giants, which other kinds of firms are rushing to emulate, delivering a severe blow to the old corporate model of traditional industrial society, which absorbed large amounts of employment. Under the operation of Coase’s theorem, how will firm size, organizational form, management models, and business processes evolve in the AI era? This evolution will not only directly affect the labor market but, transmitted through the labor market, will affect school education, household fertility and child-rearing, the reproduction of the labor force and human capital, and even the propagation of the human species. The spillover of micro-level firm effects raises macro-level social costs and will inevitably and profoundly change social organizational structures and the way they operate.
What force drives the evolution of corporate models? It is capital’s innate gene for cutting costs and raising efficiency. For an individual firm, using AI to cut costs and raise efficiency in pursuit of efficiency and profit is rational behavior endowed by the market economy; for society as a whole, however, the problem of final demand based on household purchasing power must be solved. For example, if firms produce household AI humanoid robots, who will be both willing and able to buy them? If mass unemployment leads to insufficient effective demand, and unsold products lead to a crisis of survival for firms, then does “downsizing to raise efficiency,” a concept endowed with a sense of progress and even of righteousness but in substance a profit-seeking behavior of capital, mean that firms are, in a roundabout way, slowly harming themselves and inflicting deep damage on society? And how will society respond and cope?
7. Who Is Qualified to Set the Rules for AI Development?
The AI revolution bears on the future and destiny of all humanity. Who is qualified to lay down the rules for AI development? Elon Reeve Musk’s vision of migrating to Mars is to carry on the flame of human civilization; Dario Amodei has consistently maintained that AI safety comes before corporate profit. But will the moral idealism of the tech giants bear out the famous saying that the road to hell is often paved with good intentions? On this planet of ours, which has yet to establish an effective world government, does whoever holds abundant compute and algorithms have the right to set up an “AI moral tribunal,” or even to act as the “AI world police”? (For example, Anthropic decides according to its own preferences who may use Claude.) Between tech giants and superpowers, which is more qualified, more responsible, more obligated, and more capable of ensuring that AI becomes an international public good that upholds the international order and benefits all humanity?
Whether the AI revolution brings humanity well-being or catastrophe depends on whether these seven great dilemmas can be solved, how they are solved, and to what extent they are solved.
